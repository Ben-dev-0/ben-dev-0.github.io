AI-powered bylaw evaluation for Canadian small businesses

Canada’s municipal regulations, called bylaws, are difficult for both new and experienced food entrepreneurs. The manual process of reviewing dozens extensive legal documents leads to information overload, unnececary friction, and sometimes legal trouble, when expanding or establishing in different locations.

The final deliverable analyzes documents using LLMs to generate clear and actionable insights that entrepreneurs can follow in order to comply with local laws.
