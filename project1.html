AI-powered bylaw evaluation for Canadian small businesses

Canada’s varied municipal, provincial, and territorial regulations create substantial challenges for food entrepreneurs trying to choose or expand into new locations. The manual process of reviewing extensive legal documents leads to information gaps, causing businesses to select sub-optimal sites or encounter unexpected regulatory hurdles. This project proposes an AI platform that analyzes bylaws and policies to produce comparable friendliness scores, reducing information asymmetry and enabling better location decisions.

The final deliverable analyzes documents using LLMs to generate clear and actionable insights that entrepreneurs can follow in order to comply with local laws.
